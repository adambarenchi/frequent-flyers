Frequent Flyers

Privacy Policy

Effective November 5, 2026

Frequent Flyers ("the app", "we", "us") lets friends form leagues and compare scores from daily web games. This policy explains what we collect, why, who can see it, and how to delete it. We keep it short because we collect very little.

The short version

  • We collect only what the app needs to work: your account, your profile, your scores, and your league chat.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics, and we never sell your data.
  • You can delete your account and data at any time from inside the app.

What we collect

DataWhy
Email address, and the name Apple or Google shares when you sign in with themTo create your account and sign you in. If you use Sign in with Apple and hide your email, we only receive Apple's private relay address.
Screen name and profile picture (a photo you upload, or a built-in avatar)To show who you are to other players.
Game results: the score, puzzle date, and the result text you share or paste from each gameTo score your leagues, show standings, and build your stats and awards. We keep the original text so scores can be checked and re-read if a game changes its format.
League activity: leagues you create or join, chat messages, reactionsTo run leagues and the league chat.
Safety records: players you block, and messages you reportTo hide blocked players from you and to review reported content. A report includes a copy of the reported message.
Technical logs: IP address and request times, kept by our hosting providerTo keep the service secure and working. We don't use them to track you.

We do not collect your location, contacts, browsing history, or device identifiers for advertising. The app does not read your clipboard unless you tap Paste, and it only reads text you choose to share with it.

Who can see your information

  • Other players can see your screen name and profile picture.
  • Members of your leagues can see your scores for that league (each day, other members only see your scores once they've finished their own games), your stats within the league, and your chat messages.
  • We can access the data above to operate the app, review reports, and respond to support requests.
  • Service providers process data on our behalf: Supabase hosts the database, sign-in, and file storage; Apple and Google handle sign-in if you choose them.

We don't share your data with anyone else, except where required by law.

The games themselves

Frequent Flyers isn't affiliated with the games it links to. When you tap Play, the game opens in your browser and that game's own privacy policy applies.

On your device

The app stores your sign-in session securely in the iOS Keychain, a few settings (such as whether you've seen the walkthrough), and scores waiting to upload if you're offline.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, your profile, picture, scores, league memberships, chat messages, reactions, and blocks are deleted right away. Leagues you created are handed to another member, or deleted if no one else is in them. Reports you made or that were made about you may be kept, without your account attached, so we can deal with abuse. Deleted data may remain in our hosting provider's backups for a short period before it is overwritten.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account: in the app, open your avatar menu, tap Passport, and scroll to "Delete account".
  • Change your profile: edit your screen name and picture from your Passport.
  • Access or correct your data: email us and we'll help.

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK, or California), you may have additional rights to access, correct, delete, or move your data. Email us to use them; we'll respond within 30 days.

Children

Frequent Flyers is not intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections, and database rules restrict each person to the data they're allowed to see. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above, and for significant changes we'll let you know in the app.
