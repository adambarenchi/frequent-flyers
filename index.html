Common questions

How do I submit a score?
Finish a game, tap its Share button, and pick Frequent Flyers. No Share button? Copy your result and tap Paste on the Today screen.

How do I join a league?
Ask a friend for their league's six-character code (or invite link), then tap Join on the Today screen.

Why can't I see today's scores yet?
Each day's scores stay hidden until you've played, so nobody gets spoiled. Standings count every day through yesterday.

How do I report or block someone?
Long-press their message in a league chat and choose Report or Block. You can unblock players from your avatar menu.

How do I delete my account?
Open your avatar menu, tap Passport, and scroll down to "Delete account". This permanently deletes your profile, scores, and messages.
